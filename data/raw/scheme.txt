GOVERNMENT AGRICULTURAL SCHEMES FOR WOMEN FARMERS IN MAHARASHTRA

Mahila Kisan Sashaktikaran Pariyojana (MKSP)
Description: A sub-component of the National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM). It aims to improve the present status of women in agriculture and enhance their empowerment.
Benefits: Provides women with technical training in sustainable agriculture, financial support, and capacity building. It prioritizes women-headed households and gives access to gender-friendly tools to reduce physical drudgery.
Eligibility: Women farmers, especially from SC/ST, landless, or resource-poor households.

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
Description: A central sector scheme providing direct income support to landholding farmers.
Benefits: Eligible farmers receive ₹6,000 annually, disbursed in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each directly into their bank accounts.
Eligibility: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their name. Registration can be done via the PM-Kisan portal or Common Service Centers (CSC).

Mini Tractor and Agricultural Mechanization Subsidy (SMAM)
Description: A scheme to reduce manual labor and improve farm productivity through mechanization.
Benefits: Under the Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM) and Maharashtra state schemes, women farmers are given priority. Women and marginal farmers can receive up to a 50% to 90% subsidy on mini tractors and farm equipment.
Application: Farmers can apply online through the MahaDBT portal (mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in) under the Agriculture Mechanization section.

Solar Krishi Pump Subsidy
Description: Provided by the Maharashtra Agriculture Department to promote eco-friendly irrigation and spraying, reducing reliance on costly diesel.
Benefits: High subsidies on solar-powered water pumps and spray pumps.
Application: Applications are processed through the MahaDBT portal. Required documents include Aadhaar, 7/12 land extract, and bank details.

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
Description: Financing facility for post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets.
Benefits: Provides loans with a maximum interest rate of 9%, coupled with an interest subvention (discount) of 3% per annum for loans up to ₹2 crore. Special emphasis and priority are given to women farmers and women-led Self Help Groups (SHGs).

PMFBY – Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)
Description: The government's flagship crop insurance scheme protecting farmers against natural disasters, crop failure, and pest/disease attacks.
Application: Can be applied via pmfby.gov.in. Needs 7/12 extract, sowing certificate, and bank passbook.
